ABOUT SMARTGOV

What is SmartGov?

SmartGov is an AI-powered online grievance management system built for citizens of Tamil Nadu. It bridges the gap between citizens and government departments by providing a single, unified platform to report civic problems, attach location-verified evidence, and track every update all in real time.

Whether it is a broken road, water shortage, drainage overflow, electricity failure, or a municipal issue SmartGov ensures your complaint reaches the right department instantly and does not get lost in paperwork.

AI-Powered Routing Complaints are automatically categorised and sent to the correct department using machine learning no manual sorting, no delays.
OCR Geo-Tag Verification Every photo is scanned using Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to confirm it contains real location and date data. No fake reports accepted.
Full Transparency Every status change, officer action, and department response is logged and visible to the citizen in real time on their personal dashboard.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Why SmartGov is Different

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning automatically classifies every complaint by category and assigns a priority level ensuring urgent issues like flooding or power outages get immediate attention.

Photo Verification

Using Optical Character Recognition, the system verifies that uploaded photos are genuine geo-tagged images containing visible location coordinates, date and time preventing false reports.

Location Intelligence

Every complaint is pinned to exact GPS coordinates. Department officers can open the location directly in Google Maps enabling faster on-ground response without guesswork.

Real-Time Tracking

Citizens receive status updates on their account dashboard whenever an officer updates their complaint keeping them fully informed at every stage.

MySQL Database

All citizen data, complaints, officer records, and status updates are stored securely in a MySQL database ensuring data integrity, reliability, and fast retrieval at scale.

Role-Based Access

Three separate portals Citizen, Department Officer, and Admin ensure that each user sees only what is relevant to their role, with full security and accountability.

Technology Behind SmartGov

SmartGov is built using modern, production-grade technologies to ensure reliability, security, and scalability.

Python & Flask Backend API, routing, authentication, session management and all business logic runs on Python 3 with Flask web framework.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you get started with SmartGov.

Is SmartGov free to use?
Yes. SmartGov is completely free for all citizens. There are no registration fees, no subscription charges, and no hidden costs. It is a government-supported public service platform.
What kind of photo do I need to upload?
You must upload a photo taken with a GPS Camera app (such as "GPS Map Camera") that shows the location address, GPS coordinates, date and time visibly on the image. Photos that lack embedded location EXIF data are NOT accepted.
How long does it take to resolve a complaint?
Resolution time depends on the department and issue type. High priority complaints (flooding, electrical hazards) are typically acknowledged within hours. General complaints may take 3-7 working days. You can track status updates on your dashboard throughout.
Can I track my complaint after submitting?
Yes. Once you submit a complaint, you receive a unique Reference ID (e.g. SGRS-000001). Log into your dashboard at any time to see the full status timeline, department updates, and resolution notes.
Which areas does SmartGov serve?
SmartGov currently serves citizens across the Coimbatore district of Tamil Nadu. It covers all panchayats, town panchayats, and municipal areas under the supported government departments.
What if my complaint is not resolved?
If a complaint remains unresolved beyond the expected time, it is flagged for administrator review. You can also contact the department directly using the contact details below.
